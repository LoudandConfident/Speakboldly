YOUR PACE. YOUR POSSIBILITIES.

How it works

01 — THE SESSIONS

Sessions are held online and are available for learners 16+.

We work on speaking, listening, reading, and some writing through conversation-based, fully interactive 90-minute sessions, held weekly or bi-weekly depending on your program.

02 — HOW TO GET READY

You don’t need much to get started.

  • Download ZOOM or Microsoft Teams
  • Have a stable internet connection
  • Make sure your device’s camera is clean — yes, wipe that camera well :)
  • Most importantly, come ready to talk.

Then:

Describe your needs thoroughly.
Take the placement test.
Set your target goal with us.
Build the journey needed to get there.

03 — REGISTRATION & PAYMENT

Once your registration is confirmed, payment should be completed within 24 hours of registration.

Send your bank transfer or InstaPay screenshot to:

mira.nasser.louis.saleh@gmail.com

You’ll receive your payment and registration confirmation within 24 hours.

No Refunds - No freeze options within the round (1 round = 4 weeks) - No same-day cancellations - Only one-time cancellation allowed per two rounds (8 weeks)

This is for your progress first, and our time second :)

04 — HOMEWORK

Practice makes progress.

You’ll be asked to practice English throughout the week — not just during our sessions.

This may include sending voice messages, completing activities, responding to prompts, or using English in situations connected to your everyday life.

The goal is simple:

English shouldn’t stay inside the session.

05 — MID-LEVEL PROGRESS REPORT

At the middle of your level, you’ll receive a detailed progress report.

It will help you see:

  • What you’ve developed
  • What you’re currently working on
  • What still needs attention
  • Where you should focus next

It’s not about comparing you to anyone else.

It’s about seeing your own progress clearly.

06 — LEVEL EXAMINATION

At the end of each level, you’ll take a short assessment to make sure you’ve achieved approximately 60% or more of the goals you set for that level.

The assessment is designed to measure your progress and help determine what comes next.

Examinations do not apply to workshops.

OUR VISION

A voice you feel confident using.

Our target is to make you think in English — not study it.

Since 2018, I’ve taught in more than 7 international English centers, working with foreign learners, adults, and children — both online and offline. My students have come from different backgrounds, including ministry employees, parents of fluent children, and teenagers and young adults struggling to get the opportunities they deserve.

But through all these years, one thing has consistently moved me:
The need.

I have deep respect for our native language here in Egypt, and I understand the reality of having to learn a second language simply to keep up with an increasingly demanding market.

Being bilingual can be enlightening.
But getting there can be a burden.

And I’ve come to fall in love with carrying that burden with people.

This is a safe place to:

  • Make mistakes without being judged.
  • Speak without being ashamed of your native accent until it progresses.
  • Follow a program customized according to what you actually need.
  • Learn privately, speak freely, and keep going until you reach what you came for.
  • Track real, consistent progress — at your own pace.

You just need the right environment, the right guidance, and someone willing to walk the process with you.
