General English Speaking & Conversation Course

Private English coaching tailored to your level and everyday needs.

Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced8 sessions per month or 4 sessions per monthOnline coaching

Course description

Build everyday English through two private sessions a week, with practice tailored to your level and goals. Work on speaking, listening, vocabulary, and sentence structure using real situations and personal feedback.

Sessions centre on real situations you want to handle more comfortably, such as introducing yourself, asking questions and explaining your ideas. Your instructor adjusts the practice to your English level and goals, helping you notice useful language and build habits between sessions. This is an ongoing program; how long you continue depends on your English level.

What you’ll work on

  • Everyday conversations and useful vocabulary
  • Grammar in real communication
  • Listening and clear pronunciation
  • Personalized speaking practice

What you’ll take away

You’ll develop clearer ways to express your ideas and take part in everyday conversations. You’ll also build a practical routine for recognising mistakes and continuing your progress between sessions.

Sessions and pricing

8 sessions per month · twice a week — 3,000 EGP

4 sessions per month · once a week — 2,000 EGP

One-to-one online sessions, 90 minutes each. Prices include examinations, reports, notes and access to the materials for your level.

Before you begin

Tell us about your goals and needs. A dedicated 30-minute Zoom placement, with spoken and written tasks, helps us understand your level and personalise your coaching. The free online assessments are for your own reference.
