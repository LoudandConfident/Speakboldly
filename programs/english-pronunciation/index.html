Pronunciation workshop

Develop clearer sounds, stress, rhythm and intonation.

Advanced6 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Explore English sounds, word stress, rhythm, and intonation through six practical sessions. Apply pronunciation feedback to short exchanges and connected speech.

Practise individual sounds alongside connected speech, word stress and sentence rhythm. The goal is clearer, more confident communication; your instructor helps you notice patterns and practise them in meaningful conversation.

What you’ll work on

  • Accent and pronunciation development

What you’ll take away

You’ll develop greater awareness of the patterns that help make your speech clearer. You’ll also learn a listening-and-recording routine for reviewing and refining your pronunciation independently.
