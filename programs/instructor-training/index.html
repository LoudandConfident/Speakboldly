Instructor training

Practical training for instructors with 0–2 years of experience.

For English instructors · 0–2 years of experience8 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Build practical teaching skills through eight sessions on lesson planning, classroom communication, and learner participation. Designed for instructors with 0–2 years of experience, admission requires an interview and an English exam score of at least 93%.

After more than 8 years working at Berlitz, Ucan, Oxford Academy, Kayan Academy and Bright Bridges, I am more than happy to share my experience with instructors who share the same vision and want to support others for a living. The course explores practical lesson planning, classroom communication and ways to help learners participate. Applicants must complete an interview and English exam before joining, with a minimum exam score of 93%.

What you’ll work on

  • Beginner teachers · 0–2 years of experience

What you’ll take away

You’ll practise planning purposeful lessons, giving clear instructions, and helping learners take part. You’ll also develop ways to review your teaching, use feedback, and continue improving independently.
