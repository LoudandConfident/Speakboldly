Meetings, presentation and public speaking workshop

Speak confidently in meetings, presentations and public settings.

Intermediate and advanced5 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Develop your English for meetings, presentations, and public speaking through five practical sessions. Rehearse contributions, organise your message, and handle questions with feedback on clarity and delivery.

Practise introducing a topic, linking ideas and making your point without losing your audience. Work through meeting contributions, presentation openings and question-and-answer situations, with feedback on clarity, delivery and pronunciation.

What you’ll work on

  • Meeting contributions and professional discussions
  • Presentation structure and delivery
  • Public speaking confidence
  • Handling questions and audience engagement

What you’ll take away

You’ll practise making your point clearly and supporting it with useful examples. You’ll also develop a repeatable preparation routine for presenting ideas and responding to an audience.
