Course description

Practise English for technical explanations, troubleshooting, and project discussions across eight sessions. Use situations and vocabulary relevant to your work in information technology.

Practise explaining a technical issue, describing how a system works and sharing progress with colleagues. Sessions can focus on troubleshooting conversations, project updates and technical vocabulary relevant to your work.

What you’ll work on

  • English for IT

What you’ll take away

You’ll develop clearer ways to explain problems, describe systems, and share progress with colleagues. You’ll also practise checking understanding and organising technical information for different listeners.
