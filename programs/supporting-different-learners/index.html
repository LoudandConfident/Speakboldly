The different learners

Explore learning preferences and ways to support different learners.

For instructors · Understanding learning preferences8 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Explore how visual examples, conversation, reading, and hands-on activities can support different learners. Across eight sessions, practise adapting the same topic in several ways while keeping learning preferences flexible.

Explore how pictures, spoken explanations, written examples and practical activities can support learning. The course encourages flexible teaching rather than limiting a learner to one fixed type, with ideas for presenting and practising the same topic in different ways.

What you’ll work on

  • Understanding learning preferences

What you’ll take away

You’ll develop a wider range of activities and clearer ways to respond to learners’ needs. You’ll also learn to observe what helps, gather feedback, and adjust your approach as learners progress.
