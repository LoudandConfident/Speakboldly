English Interview Preparation Workshop

Prepare clear answers and practise for your next interview.

Intermediate and advanced2 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Prepare for English-language interviews through two focused sessions of practice and personal feedback. Rehearse your introduction, common questions, and examples that show your experience.

Practise describing your experience, strengths and achievements in your own words. Work on focused answers, follow-up questions and the questions you want to ask an employer, using mock interviews and feedback to prepare.

What you’ll work on

  • Your professional introduction
  • Answers to common interview questions
  • Explaining achievements with examples
  • Mock interviews and personalized feedback

What you’ll take away

You’ll develop more focused answers and clearer ways to explain your strengths and achievements. You’ll also learn how to rehearse independently and handle follow-up questions with greater confidence.

Sessions and pricing

2 sessions — 700 EGP

One-to-one online sessions, 90 minutes each. Prices include examinations, reports, notes and access to the materials for your level.

Before you begin
