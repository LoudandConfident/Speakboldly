Reclaim your focus workshop

Build practical habits for a calmer, more focused learning routine.

For learners who want better focus and study habits8 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Across eight sessions, explore how to organise your time, reduce everyday distractions and create realistic study routines. Try practical approaches to starting tasks, taking breaks and returning your attention when it drifts.

What you’ll work on

  • Understanding everyday distractions
  • Planning manageable tasks and study time
  • Building supportive routines and breaks
  • Reviewing and adjusting your approach

What you’ll take away

You’ll build a personal toolkit for more intentional learning and work. You’ll also learn to adjust your routines around your needs and responsibilities.
