Business Email & Professional Writing Workshop

Write clear, professional emails and business messages.

Intermediate and advanced5 sessionsOnline coaching

Course description

Practise professional emails and business messages across five sessions using situations relevant to your work. Explore tone, structure, requests, and follow-ups while revising drafts with instructor feedback.

Bring examples of the kinds of messages you need to write, such as requests, updates or follow-ups. Practise adapting your tone to the reader, arranging key information clearly and revising drafts with instructor feedback.

What you’ll work on

  • Professional emails and appropriate tone
  • Requests, follow-ups and replies
  • Clear structure and concise writing
  • Editing and practical writing feedback

What you’ll take away

You’ll develop clearer messages that suit your reader and make the next action easy to understand. You’ll also build an editing checklist to review your writing before sending it.

Sessions and pricing

1,500 EGP

One-to-one online sessions, 90 minutes each. Prices include examinations, reports, notes and access to the materials for your level.
