English Momentum: Intensive English Coaching

Four days a week, one hour each, for four weeks.

Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced16 sessions · 4 weeksOnline coaching

Course description

Make English a regular part of your week with 16 private, one-hour sessions over four weeks. Your instructor adapts the practice to your level, goals, and the English you need in everyday life.

What you’ll work on

  • Frequent speaking and listening practice
  • Useful vocabulary and grammar in conversation
  • Focused feedback and revision
  • Independent practice between sessions

What you’ll take away

You’ll practise more frequently, review useful language regularly, and build habits that help you keep improving between sessions.

Sessions and pricing

16 sessions · 4 days a week · 1 hour each · 4 weeks — 3,500 EGP

One-to-one online sessions, 90 minutes each. Prices include examinations, reports, notes and access to the materials for your level.

Before you begin

Tell us about your goals and needs. A dedicated 30-minute Zoom placement, with spoken and written tasks, helps us understand your level and personalise your coaching. The free online assessments are for your own reference.
