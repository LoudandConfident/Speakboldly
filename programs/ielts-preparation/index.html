Course description

Prepare for IELTS through focused practice in listening, reading, writing, and speaking. Explore typical task formats, timed activities, and strategies shaped around the areas you need to strengthen.

Work through typical task formats and timed practice while identifying which skills need the most attention. Sessions cover organising written answers, explaining opinions in speaking tasks and approaching listening and reading questions.

What you’ll work on

  • Listening and reading strategies
  • Writing structure and clear arguments
  • Speaking practice and personalized feedback
  • Timed practice and exam preparation

What you’ll take away

You’ll build familiarity with exam tasks and practise organising answers within the available time. You’ll also learn how to review your performance and plan more focused independent preparation.
