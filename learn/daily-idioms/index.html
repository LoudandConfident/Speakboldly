Your journey, step by step

Here’s how it goes

We help you take charge of your own learning through building the skills and habits that will keep you improving independently.

01

The sessions

Sessions are virtual via Zoom and are available for learners 16+.

We work on speaking, listening, reading, and some writing through conversation-based, fully interactive 90-minute sessions, held once or twice a week depending on your program.

02

Talk to us!

Share your goals and needs, and take a spoken and written assessment.

We’ll build your plan and recommend the program that suits you best.

03

Registration & payment

Full payment is required in advance unless an alternative arrangement has been discussed with and approved by your instructor.

Once you receive your plan and scheduled days, complete payment within 24 hours. Email your payment screenshot, name and program type to billing@speakboldly.space.

You’ll receive your payment and registration confirmation within 24 hours.

SB Policy

04

Homework

You’ll be asked to practice English throughout the week.

This may include sending voice messages or completing other activities.

The goal is simple:

English shouldn’t stay inside the session.

Don’t make me use my~ Teacher Voice ~

05

Progress reports & examinations

At the middle of your level, you’ll receive a detailed progress report showing what you’ve achieved and what to work on next.

At the end of each level, you’ll take a short assessment to check whether you’ve achieved approximately 60% or more of your goals for that level and help determine what comes next.

Examinations do not apply to workshops.

06

Your next-level action plan

Have you ever finished a course and felt your progress is not as expected?

Bam. Right there. This is where people stop!

Here, we finished the first level, to send you the Action plan you need! A studied and monitored action plan tailored to your weaknesses, and watch them demolish in the next one!

OUR VISION

We want to take you where you
don’t need us anymore.

Since 2018, I’ve taught at more than seven international English centers, both online and in person. I’ve worked with ministry employees, foreigners, parents of children who speak fluent English and recent graduates looking for their first job.

Most classes left a mark in my heart. I can still remember names, laughs, tears, and milestones achieved.

Moments from the journey

Through all these years, one thing has consistently moved me:
The need.

I've come to fall in love with carrying the burden of being obliged to be bilingual to earn a Job in a demanding market!

I saw people, not salaries. I needed my own space to give them everything I had to offer.

Our goal is not merely commercial.

In fact, one of our dreams is to support people who cannot afford English coaching—in Egypt, and soon in other countries too.

FAQ
Do I need to know English before joining?

We assess your current level and recommend a suitable program based on your needs.

How long is each session?

Each private online session lasts 90 minutes.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions are held online via Zoom.

Will I have the same instructor?

Yes. Your instructor stays with you throughout the course.

How will I know I'm improving?

You'll receive individual feedback, a mid-level progress report and an end-of-level assessment where applicable.

Will I receive homework?

Yes. You'll be encouraged to practise between sessions through speaking tasks and other activities.

How much does a course cost?

Prices depend on the program. You can review the available pricing details or request a personalized quote.

Can I reschedule a session?

Rescheduling is subject to the cancellation policy explained during registration.

How do I register?

Contact us, share your goals, complete the required assessment and receive a suitable program recommendation.
