It's never too late!

Here's a joke. I started learning Ballet as a 27-year-adult.

Not only was I too old to begin, but also I was a bit overweight, with torn cruciate knee ligament and hurt ankles. I was the chubbiest in class..

But guess what? I was the best in class with the moves and the dances!

My body memorized the music and moves so well that I felt like I wanted to do this since I was a kid! Why didn't I start younger? I don't know and I don't care.

All I know is today. And today I choose to do what I love, free as a bird as if the word "Obstacles" never existed in my dictionary.

Choose to do the same. The word "late" doesn’t apply to all people. Don’t let people frustrate you on when you’ve started..what matters is that you’re brave enough to have started.

There’s grace in those journeys where we begin from where we are, and exert all the effort we could.
