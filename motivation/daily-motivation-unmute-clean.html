Speak Boldly

Daily Motivation

Unmute yourself. You have something to say. Say it in English.

Your voice deserves to be heard. Pick something you care about and say a little about it today, using the English you have. Start with a sentence you can say comfortably, then add a little more when you feel ready. It’s okay to pause, rephrase, or tell someone you need a moment. Your thoughts don’t become less valuable because you are still learning how to express them. Give yourself a chance to be heard, one conversation at a time.

Try this today: say one thought aloud in English that you would usually keep to yourself. Repeat it slowly if that helps, then add a reason or an example. Give your voice a little more space.
