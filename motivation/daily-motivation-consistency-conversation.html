Some things an English coach wants you to know!

Even I need consistency!

I’m at my most fluent when I’m teaching the most classes. Why? Because practice is a rule none of us is exempt from—not even your instructor.

Being fluent doesn’t mean finding every word instantly or knowing the vocabulary for every topic. We all pause, forget, and learn something new.

So don’t worry when the words take a moment. Keep giving yourself reasons to use them.
